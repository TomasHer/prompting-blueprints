## Main post

POV: You ask your AI assistant to "support the Product Owner" — without any context engineering.

It congratulated the gentleman with the inflatable crown on his promotion to CEO of the Product and booked his guitar for the next roadmap review. 👑🎸

Jokes aside, this photo is accidentally the best explainer of AI for Product Owners I own:

1️⃣ "IT'S IN THE BACKLOG" is not context.
An AI supporting a PO is only as good as the context you engineer for it: not just the backlog, but the priorities, the stakeholder map, and the *why* behind every decision. Skip that, and you get confidently generated nonsense. (See: crown.)

2️⃣ An inflatable guitar demos surprisingly well.
So does an AI without grounding — impressive on stage, no sound in production. Context engineering is what turns the prop into an instrument.

3️⃣ Stakeholder alignment is still a human sport.
AI will happily draft your user stories, digest 40 pages of meeting notes, and prep the steering deck. But getting the king and the keeper of the backlog to agree on one roadmap? Still your job. The AI just makes sure everyone is arguing about the same version of the truth — which, honestly, is half the battle.
