# LinkedIn PC Announcement — Template & Process

## Intent
A repeatable way to announce a new program committee (PC) role on LinkedIn **and** turn the post into a call for papers (CfP) for the track. Use it every time a role from the [Program Committee & Track Leadership Overview](./index.md) is confirmed.

**Why this format:** the CAIN 2026 Industry Track post built on it reached ~3,000 impressions and was the second-best post of the year. The CAIN 2027 post reused the structure (see [Worked example](#worked-example-cain-2027-industry-track)).

## Use when
- You have been invited to (or are returning to) a PC, track chair, or workshop committee.
- The track has an open CfP, ideally with **2–4 weeks left** before the deadline. That creates urgency without being too late for authors.

---

## Process

### 1. Collect inputs
| Input | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Conference name, edition number, co-location | Conference home page / banner |
| Dates and city (+ flag emoji) | Conference banner or home page |
| Track name and your role | Committee page on researchr (or equivalent) |
| Submission deadline (incl. time zone, e.g. AoE) | Track CfP → *Important Dates* |
| Submission types and page limits | Track CfP |
| What's new this year | Compare the CfP with last year's (review model, invited talks, proceedings, format) |
| Track chairs and PC members, with affiliations | Committee page |
| CfP link and submission link | Track page (submission link may come later) |
| Banner image | Conference home page header |
| Own promo link (e.g. DAiTA use cases) | `https://www.post.at/en/g/c/daita` |

> Verify every fact against the official pages. Aggregator sites often show deadlines without saying which track they belong to.

### 2. Draft from the template
Fill the [template](#template) below. Keep the section order: the first 2–3 lines are all that show before *…see more*, so they must carry the role, the conference, and the invitation.

### 3. Tag deliberately
- **Tag people, not companies.** Tag the track chairs and every PC member; put their organisation in plain text in brackets. Tagged people get notified, and their engagement pushes the post into their networks.
- Pick each `@name` from LinkedIn's dropdown, or the tag stays plain text. If someone does not appear (often because you are not connected), leave the name untagged.
- Don't tag yourself, and don't tag people unrelated to the track. Long tag lists with no engagement look spammy.

### 4. Publish
- Attach the conference banner as the image.
- Open each link once before posting.
- Post on a weekday morning (CET), while the "X weeks left" line is true.

### 5. Follow up
- When the submission link is published (or a week before the deadline), add it as a **comment**, thanking the chairs. That brings the post back into feeds.
- Add the engagement to the future table in [index.md](./index.md) if it isn't there yet.
- Record impressions in the [Results log](#results-log).

## Template

Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Delete optional sections (marked *opt.*) that don't apply.

```text
📣 Happy to share that I'm [back on / joining] the Program Committee for the [TRACK NAME] of #[CONF][YEAR], the [Nth] [FULL CONFERENCE NAME][, co-located with #[HOST][YEAR]]. If you're [WHO SHOULD SUBMIT, e.g. building AI systems in production], I'd like to read your work 📝.

When & where: [DD–DD Month YYYY] • [City, Country] [FLAG]

⏳ Just over [N] weeks left to submit. Deadline: [D Mon YYYY] ([TIME ZONE])

🗂️ Submission types we're inviting:
- [TYPE 1] ([LIMIT])
- [TYPE 2] ([LIMIT])
- [TYPE 3] ([LIMIT])

💡 What we're looking for: [ONE-SENTENCE SCOPE FROM THE CFP].

🆕 New this year:  (opt.)
- [CHANGE 1]
- [CHANGE 2]

👉 Call for papers & scope: [CFP URL]

🏭 Why this matters to me: [ONE OR TWO SENTENCES LINKING YOUR WORK TO THE TRACK]. See some of our use cases: https://www.post.at/en/g/c/daita

🙏 Track Co-Chairs: @[CHAIR 1] ([ORG]) and @[CHAIR 2] ([ORG])

✅ Program Committee: Joining a great group of experts, including @[MEMBER] ([ORG]), @[MEMBER] ([ORG]), …, and @[MEMBER] ([ORG]).

#[CONF][YEAR] #[HOST][YEAR] #[TOPIC TAGS…] #CFP
```

### Reuse with an AI assistant
Paste this prompt together with the CfP text, a screenshot of the committee page, and the conference banner:

```text
Fill the LinkedIn PC announcement template below for me.
- My role: [ROLE] on [TRACK] at [CONFERENCE].
- Use only facts from the attached CfP, committee page, and banner. Mark anything you cannot confirm as [BRACKETS].
- Compute "weeks left" from today's date ([TODAY]) to the deadline.
- Tag chairs and all PC members (excluding me) as @Name (Org); orgs as plain text.
- Keep the template's section order, emojis, and length.
[PASTE TEMPLATE]
```

## Worked example: CAIN 2027 Industry Track
Posted October 2026, ~3 weeks before the 6 Nov 2026 deadline.

```text
📣 Happy to share that I'm back on the Program Committee for the Industry Track of #CAIN2027, the 6th International Conference on AI Engineering, co-located with #ICSE2027. If you're building AI systems in production, I'd like to read your work 📝.

When & where: 25–26 April 2027 • Dublin, Ireland 🇮🇪

⏳ Just over 3 weeks left to submit. Deadline: 6 Nov 2026 (AoE)

🗂️ Submission types we're inviting:
- AI Engineering in Practice (up to 10+2 pages)
- Extended Abstracts (up to 5 pages)
- Talk Proposals (1 page + up to 10 slides)

💡 What we're looking for: real-world challenges in building AI-enabled systems, lessons learned, and practices that worked (or didn't). The focus is software engineering for AI, not algorithms.

🆕 New this year:
- Invited talks and interactive sessions
- No double-blind review: your company and context are visible, so reviewers can judge the real industrial relevance
- Papers are published in the ICSE Companion proceedings

👉 Call for papers & scope: https://conf.researchr.org/track/cain-2027/cain-2027-industry-track

🏭 Why this matters to me: At DAiTA, Austrian Post Business Solutions, we take AI from idea to production, and the hard part is rarely the model. It's the engineering around it, which is exactly what this track is about. See some of our use cases: https://www.post.at/en/g/c/daita

🙏 Track Co-Chairs: @Nadia Nahar (Carnegie Mellon University) and @Jan-Philipp Steghöfer (XITASO)

✅ Program Committee: Joining a great group of experts, including @Titus Barik (Apple), @Scott Barnett (Deakin University), @Christian Berger (Chalmers), @Priyank Desai (Amazon), @Mateus Devino, @Darko Durisic, @Smita Ghaisas, @Andrew Habib (ABB), @K. Eric Harper, @Kentaro Yoshimura (Hitachi), @Karthik Vaidhyanathan (IIIT Hyderabad), and @Marc Zeller (Siemens).

#CAIN2027 #ICSE2027 #AIEngineering #SE4AI #IndustryTrack #CFP #SoftwareEngineering #MLOps #TrustworthyAI #GenAI
```

**What changed from the CAIN 2026 post:** opened with the role, replaced the topics list (the 2027 CfP defers topics to the Scope page) with a one-line scope plus a *New this year* section, added the DAiTA promo, and tagged all PC members instead of only companies.
